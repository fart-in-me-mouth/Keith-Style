# Evidence, provenance, and limits

## What this profile is based on

Prepared October 29, 2026 from accessible Keith-related conversations and files, including essay drafting/revision chains, earlier writings explicitly supplied as his own, and direct style instructions. The underlying research distinguished the native Library `/Keith` folder from a separate Keith ChatGPT Project; search results did not reliably expose project membership.

The scoped non-image folder inventory contained 368 items. That is an inventory count, not a claim that 368 original writings were read. Six relevant essay/summary PDFs were read, with substantial version overlap; those are not six independent authorship samples. Research also examined multiple chat contexts and recovered five older writing blocks. It did not establish an exhaustive reading of every Keith chat.

A shared account can contain other people's writing. Neither appearing in search nor being in a user-role message proves Keith wrote the prose. Some user messages contain pasted assistant drafts, source essays, classmates' writing, or jointly authored work. A `model_generated` metadata flag is not a reliable authorship determination.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. **Current explicit instruction** controls the current task and can override historical preferences.
2. **Directly requested revisions and repeated explicit preferences** strongly establish what Keith wants the assistant to preserve or change in that context.
3. **Text explicitly identified as his unedited or past writing** supports composition patterns, subject to user-reported provenance and possible collaboration.
4. **Final user-revised AI-assisted text** supports preference and final voice; focus on what he changed. Retained assistant wording is weaker evidence of native habits.
5. **Approval of assistant wording** shows acceptance, not independent authorship.
6. **Uncertain account attribution, generated books, copied assignments, peer responses, or source text** do not train the core voice.

Repeated versions do not multiply confidence. Absence from the accessible corpus does not mean Keith never uses a form. No quantitative frequency or accuracy claim was established.

## Confidence by feature

**Strong:** preserve the user's wording and intentional revisions; prefer direct explanation over fluff; do not confuse concision with deleting reasoning; separate sample style from sample content; distinguish professional formality and tact from conversational informality and argumentative bluntness; avoid imposed emotional framing; respect the explicit no-em-dash preference in comparable essay contexts without generalizing it to every genre.

**Supported tendencies, not laws:** multi-clause causal sentences, repeated topic nouns, explicit statements of judgment, concession followed by a reasoned response, concrete consequences, occasional rhetorical emphasis or narrative humor.

**Limited or inferential:** the full professional register, technical documentation, creative narration, and unfamiliar genres. Use a conservative adaptation of the core plus the current task, not a claim of exact replication. No evidence supports universal profanity, hostility, detachment, sloppy grammar, or a fixed paragraph/sentence length.

## Version 4 calibration record

Review of the subsequently supplied v3 article found that its professional register survived but paragraph cohesion regressed relative to the supplied v2 output. Examples included isolated “However, their explanations differ.” and “Jesus of Nazareth lived within this environment.” before the sentences that developed them. This is observed behavior in generated samples, not a controlled attribution of the regression to the package. Version 4 makes paragraph construction and the final paragraph-break check more operational instead of adding stronger formality rules or suppressing uncertainty. The worked example is synthetic. Test results remain limited to their actual prompts and outputs; Keith's future edits carry more weight.

## Exclusions and privacy

Do not import unrelated people's writing, generated book prose, third-party essays, classroom peers' posts, or uncertain-account material into authentic examples. Do not include account identifiers, private contact details, or irrelevant relationship data in new drafts. These source links identify private chats; they may not open for another reader and should not be made public casually. The skill is usable from its included instructions and excerpts without access to the chats.

## Updating the profile

When Keith changes a draft, retain the before/after pair, genre, immediate instruction, and authorship label. Separate changed wording from inherited AI wording. Look for recurring decisions across relevant contexts, but allow a one-off current instruction to govern its own task. Promote a new tendency only when the evidence supports it; do not accumulate prohibitions from every local correction.

## Skill-design references

The portable structure follows the [Agent Skills specification](https://agentskills.io/specification): a focused `SKILL.md` entrypoint with conditional supporting references. Public writing skills such as [deslop](https://github.com/stephenturner/skill-deslop/blob/main/SKILL.md) and [Humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer/blob/main/SKILL.md) motivate an editorial diagnose → revise → audit approach. Their generic stylistic rules are not evidence about Keith and are not imported as universal bans. Personal samples and direct corrections take priority over anti-AI folklore.
